Body Mass Index(BMI)

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a simple indicator that helps determine whether a person’s weight is appropriate for their height. It is often used as a first step in assessing overall body condition and potential health risks.

Calculating BMI takes only a few seconds, but it can indicate whether a person is underweight, within a normal range, or overweight.

An interesting fact is that BMI was developed in the 19th century and is still widely used today. However, it does not take muscle mass or fat distribution into account, so it is not a perfect measure. BMI is best viewed as a starting point and should be considered alongside lifestyle, physical activity, and overall well-being.
