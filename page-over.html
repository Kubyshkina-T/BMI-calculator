Overweight

A Body Mass Index (BMI) of 25 or higher indicates overweight. This means that body weight exceeds the recommended range for a given height and may place additional strain on the body.

With an elevated BMI, the load on the joints, cardiovascular system, and metabolism increases. Over time, this may raise the risk of chronic fatigue and metabolic disorders. However, BMI alone does not reflect fat distribution or muscle mass and does not always provide a complete picture of physical fitness.

It is important to remember that overweight is not only a matter of appearance, but primarily a matter of health and quality of life. Weight management should be gradual and safe, without extreme dieting or severe restrictions.

If your BMI is above the normal range, it is recommended to focus on sustainable lifestyle changes: balanced nutrition, regular physical activity, and the development of healthy habits. Gradual weight reduction can improve overall well-being and reduce strain on the body.
