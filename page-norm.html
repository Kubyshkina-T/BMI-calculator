Normal Body Mass Index

A Body Mass Index (BMI) between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered normal. This means that body weight generally corresponds to height and the body is in a balanced state.

With a normal BMI, body weight usually does not place excessive strain on the joints, heart, or internal organs. Metabolism tends to function steadily, and health risks associated with being underweight or overweight are considered minimal.

It is important to understand that a normal BMI represents a range, not a single “ideal” number. People with the same BMI can look very different, as this index does not take into account body composition, muscle mass, fat distribution, or individual body characteristics.

If your BMI falls within the normal range, the focus should not be on weight loss, but on maintaining overall health and body quality. A balanced diet, sufficient protein intake, regular physical activity, and adequate sleep help support energy levels, muscle tone, and overall well-being.

A normal BMI is a helpful guideline indicating that your weight is within a healthy range. For a more accurate assessment of physical condition, it is also important to consider activity level, body composition, and how you feel overall.
